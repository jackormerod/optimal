# Food data and software

CoFID: Public Health England, McCance and Widdowson's Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset, 2021. Crown copyright, Open Government Licence v3.0. Original: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/composition-of-foods-integrated-dataset-cofid . Licence: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/ . Values are per 100g. Missing and trace values are retained as unknown; no missing micronutrient is filled with zero. The bundled JSON is generated from the official workbook by scripts/import-cofid.py.

Open Food Facts: https://world.openfoodfacts.org . Database under ODbL, contents under Database Contents License. Product information is contributed by users; verify against the package. Cached and corrected products remain separate from the CoFID database. No product images are copied.
